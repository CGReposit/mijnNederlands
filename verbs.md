# Essential Dutch verbs
<p class="lead">Start with the verbs Dutch uses constantly. Learn each as a four-part unit: infinitive, simple past, perfect auxiliary and participle.</p>
<div class="notice"><strong>Priority:</strong> master <em>zijn</em> and <em>hebben</em> first, followed by modal verbs and the common irregular verbs below.</div>

## Notice the pattern
- Most perfect forms use **hebben**: *Ik heb het boek gelezen.*
- Movement or change often uses **zijn**: *Ik ben naar huis gegaan.*
- A modal sends the other infinitive to the end: *Ik moet morgen werken.*
### Practice
Choose five verbs. For each, write one present sentence, one simple-past sentence and one perfect sentence. Keep the subject and meaning similar so the tense change is obvious.
