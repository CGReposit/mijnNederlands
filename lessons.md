# Beginner lessons
<p class="lead">A practical route through the 25 absolute-beginner lesson notes: learn one communicative task, notice one grammar pattern, then use it aloud.</p>
> **How to study:** read the dialogue, shadow it aloud twice, cover the English, then write three new sentences using the same pattern.

## Model dialogue
| Dutch | English |
|---|---|
| Hallo, Anna. Hoe gaat het? | Hello, Anna. How are you? |
| Goed, dank je. En met jou? | Good, thank you. And you? |
| Ook goed. Wat een verrassing! | Good too. What a surprise! |
### Pattern to reuse
- **Hoe gaat het met …?** — How is … doing?
- **Goed, dank je wel.** — Fine, thank you.
- **En met jou/u?** — And you? (informal/formal)
- **Ook goed.** — Good too.
### Your turn
Write a four-line conversation in which you greet a colleague, ask how they are and ask about another person. Then read both parts aloud.
