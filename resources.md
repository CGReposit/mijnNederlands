## A useful listening routine
1. Watch once without pausing and identify the situation.
2. Watch with subtitles and note five reusable phrases.
3. Replay short lines and imitate the rhythm aloud.
4. Retell the video in three simple Dutch sentences.
## Pronunciation check
Use [Acapela’s text-to-speech demo](https://www.acapela-group.com/demos/) to hear a sentence, then record yourself saying the same line. Compare rhythm and vowel length—not only individual consonants.
